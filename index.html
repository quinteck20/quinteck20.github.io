Report cards for basic schools in Ghana — offline

SmartGrade helps basic schools enter scores, work out results and positions, and print terminal report cards — quickly, correctly and without needing the internet. It runs on the school's own Windows computers and Android phones.

Score entry
Class work and exam scores, by class and subject.
Results & positions
Totals, grades and positions worked out for you.
Report cards
Printable, with your school's logo and signatures.
Teachers' phones
Sync with the main device over the school Wi-Fi.
Private by design
Pupil data stays on the school's devices, locked with encryption and PINs.
Backups
Automatic backups, plus the school's own Google Drive if it chooses.

Google Drive backup

If a school chooses to connect Google Drive, SmartGrade asks Google only for permission to create and read its own backup files (in a folder called "SmartGrade Backups") and to see the email address of the account, so the school knows which account is connected. SmartGrade cannot see any other files in the school's Drive. Backups go straight from the school's device to the school's own Google account — QuinTecK never receives them. See the Privacy Policy.
