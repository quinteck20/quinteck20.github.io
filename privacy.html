Privacy Policy

Version 1.0

SmartGrade keeps pupils' and staff records on the School's own devices. QuinTecK only receives the few details needed to issue licences, send SMS and process renewals. This policy explains what is kept where.

1. Who is responsible for what

  • The School decides what pupil, parent and staff information it records in SmartGrade. For that information the School is the data controller and is responsible for using it lawfully, including under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
  • QuinTecK provides the software. For the licensing details in section 3, QuinTecK is the data controller.

2. Data kept only on the School's devices

QuinTecK cannot see, access or recover any of this:

DataWhere it is kept
Pupils: names, class, gender, ID numbers, photos (if added)The School's devices
Scores, results, positions, attendance, remarks, report cardsThe School's devices
Staff accounts: names, usernames, PINs (stored scrambled, not readable)The School's devices
School details: logo, signature, contactsThe School's devices
Sign-in history (who signed in, when, wrong PINs)That device only
Automatic backupsThe device (Windows: Documents\SmartGrade Backups)

Pupils' and staff names and other personal fields are locked (encrypted) with a key that belongs to the School's licence. Someone who copies the database file without the licence cannot read them.

Sync between the School's devices happens directly over the School's own Wi-Fi or hotspot. It never goes through the internet or through QuinTecK.

3. Data QuinTecK receives (licensing only)

WhatWhy
School nameTo issue a licence locked to that school
Phone number(s) given at trial sign-up or renewalTo send the activation code and renewal messages by SMS
Activation codes, licence numbers and datesTo activate, check and renew licences
Renewal records: plan, amount, date, Paystack referenceTo confirm renewals and keep business records
The version of these terms the School accepted, and whenTo keep a record of the agreement

QuinTecK does not receive pupil, parent or staff data, scores, or report cards.

4. Services we use

  • Supabase (online database) stores the licensing details in section 3.
  • Paystack processes payments. Card and Mobile Money details are entered on Paystack's own page and never reach SmartGrade or QuinTecK.
  • Arkesel delivers SMS (activation codes, renewal confirmations).
  • Google Drive (optional): if the School connects it, backups are saved in the School's own Google account. SmartGrade can only see the backup files it created there, nothing else in that Drive. The Google sign-in is kept privately on the device and is not included in backups.

Each service has its own privacy policy.

5. Update checks

Once a week SmartGrade checks QuinTecK's download site for a newer version. This check sends no information about the School.

6. SMS

By giving a phone number at trial sign-up or renewal, the School agrees to receive SMS about its licence, codes and renewals. We do not send advertising SMS.

7. Children's data

SmartGrade is used by school staff, not by children. It holds information about pupils, many of whom are children. The School must handle that information carefully, limit access with staff accounts and PINs, and obtain any consent from parents or guardians that the law or its own rules require.

8. How long we keep licensing details

We keep the details in section 3 while the School has a licence, and then for up to 6 years for accounting and tax records. A School may ask us to delete its details sooner, except records we must keep by law.

9. Your rights

The School (and any person whose details QuinTecK holds) may ask us to see, correct or delete those details by emailing quinteck20@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. Requests about pupils' data go to the School, which controls it.

10. Security

We protect licensing details with access controls and accounts with two-step sign-in. Licences and updates are digitally signed by QuinTecK so they cannot be forged. No system is perfectly secure; the School should protect its devices and PINs too.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Each version has a number and date, and SmartGrade asks users to accept the new version.

12. Contact

QuinTecK · quinteck20@gmail.com · WhatsApp through "Contact vendor" in SmartGrade

Google user data (Google Drive backup)

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If the School connects it, SmartGrade requests only these Google permissions:

  • drive.file — to create, read and delete only the backup files SmartGrade itself makes, in a folder named "SmartGrade Backups". SmartGrade cannot see or change any other file in the School's Drive.
  • userinfo.email — to show which Google account is connected.

The backup files travel directly between the School's device and the School's own Google account. QuinTecK does not receive, store, read, sell or share them, or the School's Google sign-in. The sign-in is kept privately on the School's device. The School can disconnect at any time in SmartGrade (Backup & Restore) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the backup files from its Drive.

SmartGrade's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
